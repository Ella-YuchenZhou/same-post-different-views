    What this model can—and cannot—show

    • All comments, participant labels, like counts, and timestamps are fictional examples. No real usernames are used.
    • Most liked is a simple numerical rule, not YouTube’s real Top comments algorithm.
    • Mixed viewpoints depends on manual labels and a human-chosen category order. It is not the fairest or most accurate ranking.
    • This model shows ordering and visibility only. It does not measure attitude changes or whether information is true.
    • Four simplified categories cannot capture a comment’s full meaning. Unclear does not mean neutral; Concerned/Opposed does not mean everyone in that category wants a total ban.
    How the manual categories are defined

    Supportive: Explicitly supports AI use in coursework without making a restriction a condition of that support.

    Concerned/Opposed: Mainly expresses risks, concern, or opposition, without an explicit proposal for conditional acceptance.

    Conditional: Explicitly accepts AI use only under stated conditions. This category takes priority when concern accompanies a clear acceptance condition.

    Unclear: The text does not establish a position on AI use in coursework, including reactions to the talk alone or ambiguous remarks.
